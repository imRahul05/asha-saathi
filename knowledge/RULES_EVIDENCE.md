## Ambiguities

1. Single vs two BP readings: icmr-anc-stw requires "BP > 140/90mm Hg on 2 occasions 6 hours apart" for high-risk status. It does not say what a single reading means (no interim action, no "recheck" instruction). nhm-cho-mh and pmsma give no BP numbers at all. Code cannot cite any source for treating a single reading >= 140/90 as high risk; a single-reading rule is NOT FOUND. Any single-reading alert (e.g. "recheck in 6 hours") is a product decision, not source-backed. It is also not stated whether both occasions must exceed the threshold (implied, not said).
2. Strictly greater vs or-more: the source writes "> 140/90" (strictly greater than), so exactly 140/90 would not qualify literally. Also unclear whether the rule is systolic > 140 AND diastolic > 90, or EITHER; the source writes only "140/90mm Hg". Unclear what applies to e.g. 150/80.
3. Severe anaemia cut-off: the only numeric figures are Hb < 7 g% (listed as high risk, and refer to higher centre), Hb < 9 g% (parenteral iron) and the IFA dose split at 11 g%. No source defines mild / moderate / severe bands with numbers. pmsma and nhm-cho-mh use the words "severe anaemia" / "mild and moderate" without numbers. Reading "severe" as < 7 is an inference, not stated. Hb exactly 11, 9 or 7 is unassigned by the literal text.
4. Emergencies vs referrals: icmr-anc-stw lists "danger signals" and says refer to higher centre for "danger signals" but does not rank them or label any as an emergency; vaginal bleeding is explicitly "referred to CHC or higher centre". The only explicit "refer directly" emergency list is nhm-cho-mh (PPH, eclampsia, retained placenta, sepsis), mostly labour/postpartum conditions, so mapping antenatal danger signs (e.g. convulsion = eclampsia) to "emergency" is an inference. nhm-cho-mh says high BP, GDM or syphilis "can be referred" to a PHC/CHC with MBBS MO, a lower tier.
5. Age and parity: "Age > 35 years or < 19 years or parity > 4" is one line; ages 35 and 19 and parity exactly 4 are not high risk by the literal text. "parity" is not defined (whether it includes the current pregnancy).
6. Not found in any source: swelling of face/hands as a danger sign (only "Pedal edema" as an exam item), pre-eclampsia / severe-hypertension BP cut-offs, any foetal-movement count. Reduced foetal movement applies only "at > 28 weeks".
7. Document-version risk: the requested ICMR URL was dead; the replacement is dated December 2025, and a combined ICMR O&G volume (obg_all-2.pdf, citing "2019 Edition, Vol. 1") contains identical antenatal text. The STW is aimed at facility clinicians; its applicability to ASHA-level decisions is not stated.
