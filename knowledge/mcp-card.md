## Danger signs
Page: 4

> • If you or anyone in your family sees any of these danger signs, take the pregnant woman to the hospital immediately
> • High fever during pregnancy or within one month of delivery
> • Labour pain for more than 12 hours
> • Severe Anemia with or without breathlessness
> • Headache, blurring of vision, fits and swelling all over the body
> • Bursting of water bag without labour pains
> • Bleeding during pregnancy
> • Excessive bleeding during delivery or after delivery


## Haemoglobin / anaemia
Page: 2

> • Take one tablet of iron folic acid a day for at least 6 months after first trimester. Take at least 180 tablets.
> • Take two tablets of calcium per day for at least 6 months after 1st trimester
> • Take single dose of tablet albendazole (400 mg) after 1st trimester


## Antenatal check-ups
Page: 2

> • Register with the health centre in the 1st trimester. Have at least 3 antenatal checkups, after registration. Have blood pressure (BP) checked and blood and urine examined at each visit.
> • Gain at least 10- 12 kg. during pregnancy.
> • Ensure nutrition counselling at every ANC


## Counselling, nutrition and birth preparedness
Page: 2

> • Consume a variety of foods
> • Take at least two hours of rest during the day.
> • In addition to 8 hours of rest at night.
> • Use only adequately iodised salt

> • Ensure Institutional Delivery
> • Arrange transport to hospital
> • Arrange for transport in advance
> • Register under Janani Suraksha Yojna (JSY)
> • Ensure 48 hours of stay after delivery
